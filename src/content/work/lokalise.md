I worked on the Growth team, on the systems that turn a signup into a paying, active customer: onboarding, activation, pricing, and billing. I took an ROI Calculator from concept to release, and shipped billing and monetization features inside Flow, working closely with design.

Over time the work went deeper into infrastructure and AI. I helped build company-wide experimentation and analytics tooling, worked on Pricing V2, untangled a first-party identity architecture (including a gnarly Amplitude identity bug), and prototyped an MCP server so internal tools could talk to LLM agents. I also helped the team fold AI into how they work day to day.
