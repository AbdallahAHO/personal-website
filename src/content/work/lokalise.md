Lokalise builds software that teams use to translate and ship their products in other languages. I spent most of my time there on the Growth team, on the systems that turn a signup into a paying, active customer: onboarding, activation, pricing, and billing.

The biggest thing I built was the company's experimentation and analytics infrastructure: an upgraded analytics SDK, A/A tests to prove the numbers could be trusted, and an experiment registry with a governance framework and a runbook, so product and engineering had one way to run experiments. I also shipped the product side of the new pricing model (usage-based limits, limit notifications, a rebuilt usage overview), took an ROI Calculator from concept to release, and untangled a first-party identity setup, including a gnarly Amplitude identity bug.

In June 2026 I moved into a Product Engineer role on the team building workflow automation and AI-assisted localization. I led a cross-project task dashboard for localization managers end to end, from the API contract to release, and worked on its counterpart for reviewers: one view that sorts their tasks across projects by urgency.

AI ran through all of it. I built an MCP integration so AI assistants could work with the Lokalise platform, was active in the AI Engineering Guild, and helped teams fold AI-assisted development into how they work day to day.
